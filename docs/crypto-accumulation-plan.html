Five hundred to five thousand

You are asking how to catch alt season. The more useful answer is that alt season is not the hard part — being positioned before it, and selling into it, is. Right now the tape is roughly ten months into a bear market, which is the best entry environment you will get and the worst environment for the tool you built to find entries.

Where the tape actually is — 15 Aug 2026

Altcoin season index ~30 75 confirms alt season

Peak was October 2025 at $126k. The consensus bottom zone is $56–68k, and price is inside it — which is not the same as it being over. More than 40% of altcoins sitting at all-time lows is a deeper washout than the last bear market produced, and most of those names are never coming back.

Why the Alt Season tab isn't catching these early

It isn't broken. I read the engine — alt-cron-background.js is genuinely well-built, and the graded flag log is more honest than most paid products. But there are four structural reasons it cannot answer the question you are actually asking.

The universe is capped at the top 250 by market cap

root cause

MARKETS_URL requests per_page=250&page=1. The coins that "come out of nowhere and become the new names" enter from rank 400–1500. By the time one is inside the top 250, it has already done the 5–10x you wanted to catch. The screener is structurally incapable of seeing the thing you built it to find — everything else it does well is downstream of a universe that excludes the answer.

It is a chase-detector, and it knows it

bandOf needs freshBreak plus 24h acceleration plus positive RS vs BTC. flagTier refuses to flag the quiet band at all — deliberately, and the comment explains why (it produced 52 junk flags). That is the right call for finding ignition. It means the tool fires as a move starts, never before. "Ahead of time" was never in scope.

It will be near-silent for the next 6–12 months

PHASE_GATE ties the flag budget to the regime. At today's read the tape is btc_only or risk_off — a cap of 3–5 open flags against a score floor of 74–78. That is correct behaviour for a chase-detector in a bear market, and it is exactly the wrong behaviour for someone trying to accumulate. The quietness is a feature that is currently working against you.

The season score describes the past, it doesn't lead it

Breadth carries 60 of the 100 points, and breadth is "what already happened over the last 7 and 30 days." Fear & greed is coincident. Dominance is measured as a 30-day level change. By the time that score prints alt_season, the first 60% of the move is behind you. The genuinely leading legs in there — ETH/BTC 7d and 30d — are worth 5 points each.

The reframe — you are in the wrong phase for signals

There are only three jobs across a full cycle, and each one wants a different tool. The mistake is running the phase-two tool during phase one and concluding the tool is bad.

01

Accumulate

you are here
now → bottom confirmation, likely 3–9 months

Nothing is running, so there is nothing to chase. The job is buying survivors cheaply and sizing so you can sit through another −40%. Screening for breakouts here returns noise. Screening for survival returns the shortlist.

02

Rotate

dominance rolls over → alt season proper

Capital moves down the risk curve in a fixed order: BTC → ETH → large alts → mid-caps → small caps and memes. This is where your existing screener becomes the best tool you own — its flag log, targets and entry reads are built for exactly this tape.

03

Distribute

euphoria — the phase that decides your number

Everyone catches some of a bull market. Almost nobody sells it. This is the only phase where the difference between $1,200 and $5,000 is actually determined, and it is determined by rules written in advance — which is why the ladder below matters more than any pick.

The $500, allocated

You cannot reach 10x on a book that is mostly Bitcoin — BTC at 5x turns $500 into $2,500 and stops. You also cannot reach it on twelve micro-caps, because you will not survive the drawdowns. The structure that works is a barbell: a core that reliably captures the beta, and a tail sized so that one winner carries the book and the losers cannot hurt you.

CORE 50% SURVIVORS 30% TAIL 20%
Sleeve Size How many What qualifies Realistic at cycle top
Core $250 2 × $125 ETH and SOL. Deep liquidity, ETF bids, near-zero chance of going to zero. This is your floor, not your upside. $1,250–2,000
Survivors $150 4 × $37 Top-150 cap, down 75–90%, still doing >$20M daily volume, still shipping, no large unlock cliff ahead. $600–1,800
Tail $100 8 × $12 Sub-$300M cap in an emerging narrative, volume that held up through the washout. Assume 5–6 go to zero. $0–2,500

Fourteen positions is the ceiling, not a target. Below ~$12 a position the fees and the attention cost exceed the diversification benefit.

The answer to "there are too many coins"

Stop tracking coins. Track narratives. At any moment there are roughly 8–12 live ones — AI agents, DePIN, RWA, L2s, gaming, privacy, memes, restaking. Two or three of them produce nearly all of the outsized winners in a given cycle, and the coins inside a narrative move as a cohort, together, before any individual chart looks interesting. That turns an intractable problem — rank 1,400 coins — into a tractable one: pick 3–4 narratives, own the two most liquid names in each. It is also computable, which is build item #2 below.

How to actually get in

  • Split the core, place the tail. Buy ETH and SOL as six monthly tranches of about $42 each — you do not know where the bottom is, and averaging removes the need to. Buy the Survivors and Tail sleeves as single entries on capitulation days (a −12% day across the market), not on a schedule. Those are the days the illiquid names give you a price.
  • Use a pro venue. On $500, the retail Coinbase app's ~1.5–2.5% spread-plus-fee is a real tax; Coinbase Advanced, Kraken Pro or equivalent charge ~0.25–0.4% on maker orders. That is the difference between ~$12 and ~$3 on entry — and it compounds on every rotation you make later.
  • Self-custody the core once it's built. A hardware wallet for the ETH/SOL sleeve. You are planning to hold through a full cycle; exchanges have a poor record of surviving one.
  • Write the thesis down per position, in one line. If you cannot say why you own it in a sentence, you will not hold it through the −60% that happens even in the winners, and you will not know when it breaks.

The exit ladder — this is the part that decides the number

Everything above is ordinary. This is where almost everyone loses the trade: they get the 10x on screen and give it back, because no rule ever told them to sell. Set the ladder now, while you have no positions and no feelings about them.

One additional rule, and it is not optional: never add to a position that is already up 5×. That is the single most common way a 10× book becomes a 2× book.

The regime override — sell regardless of rung

Your own engine can already call the top, and it is the most valuable thing on the tab that you currently are not acting on. When all three of these are true at once, sell 50% of everything, ladder position irrelevant:

  • Season score prints alt_season — ≥70 on the published scale
  • Fear & greed ≥80 (extreme greed, sustained more than a week)
  • BTC dominance falling for 60+ days and the domTrend leg pinned at falling

That combination is late-stage euphoria. It has marked the distribution zone in every prior cycle, and it is roughly the last three weeks in which the exit liquidity actually exists.

What $5,000 actually requires

Straight answer, because sizing decisions depend on it. Run the sleeves above through a cycle expansion comparable to 2022→2025 and the median lands around $2,500–3,500. Hitting $5,000 needs the Tail sleeve to produce at least one genuine 30× — which is the designed outcome of that sleeve, not a fluke, but it is roughly a 60–70th-percentile result rather than a base case.

And that is all conditional on a cycle expansion happening at all. Over any 18–30 month window that is perhaps a coin flip. The left tail — no expansion, or picks that don't survive — is $300–800, and it is a real outcome, not a disclaimer.

Two implications worth taking seriously. First, the timeline is 18–30 months, not months — you said you don't need it overnight, and that is the single biggest edge you have here. Second, $500 that must become $5,000 is a different instruction than $500 you can afford to lose; the plan above assumes the latter, and no honest version of this plan assumes the former.

What to change in the Markets tab, in order

Each of these is scoped against the code as it stands, ordered by how much it moves the thing you actually asked for.

1 · Widen the universe and track rank velocity

highest value

Fetch pages 1–4 of /coins/markets instead of page 1 — top 1000, still keyless, still four calls inside the background function's 15-minute budget. Then store rank alongside price in the alt_snapshots prices map, which already writes hourly. A coin climbing rank 600 → 300 over 30 days is the strongest early tell that exists for a future leader, it is computable from data you are one column away from already having, and nothing else on the tab can see it.

2 · Add narrative cohorts

highest value

/coins/categories is one keyless call returning every category's market cap and 24h change. Ranking those by 7d and 30d change answers "which narrative is bidding" — the leading indicator that per-coin screening structurally cannot produce, because coins in a cohort move together before any single chart looks interesting. This is the direct fix for "there are too many coins to track."

3 · Add an Accumulation mode to the screener

this cycle phase

A second scoring function beside screenCoin, ranking on the inverse criteria: depth of drawdown from ATH, base width (a narrow 7d range, not a breakout), volume floor holding up rather than collapsing, and survivorship — still inside the top 300 after −85%. Gate it on the season phase so the tab shows Accumulation in risk_off/btc_only and the existing ignition board from mixed upward. That makes the tab useful in the phase you are actually in.

4 · A positions and ladder layer

converts plan to action

alt_flags grades the screener's ideas but nothing tracks yours — what you hold, cost basis, which ladder rung is next, how far price is from it. Given that the exit ladder is the part that determines whether this ends at $5,000, it is strange that it is the one part of the strategy with no representation in the app. Same table shape as alt_flags, plus a rung column.

5 · Make the season score lead instead of follow

refinement

Add stablecoin supply growth as a part — aggregate USDT + USDC market cap rising is dry powder entering the system, and it leads breadth by weeks. Raise the ETH/BTC legs above 5 points each; ETH/BTC turning up is the canonical first domino of a rotation. And measure dominance as rate-of-change rather than a 30-day level delta, so a sharp roll-over registers before the level has travelled far enough to trip DOM_FLAT_PTS.
